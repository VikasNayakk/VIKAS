### Example 2: Zomato/Swiggy Ka Model

Socho Zomato kaise kaam karta hai:

```
Restaurant (Khaana banata hai)
        ↓
   Zomato (Platform — khaana nahi banata, sirf connect karta hai)
        ↓
Customer (Khaana order karta hai, pay karta hai)
```

**Zomato kya karta hai?**
- Khaana NAHI banata
- Delivery NAHI karta (wo toh delivery boy karta hai)
- Sirf CONNECT karta hai Restaurant ↔ Customer
- Dono se commission leta hai

**TaskPaisa EXACTLY same model hai:**

```
AI Worker (Kaam karta hai — images, videos, writing)
        ↓
   TaskPaisa (Platform — kaam NAHI karta, sirf connect karta hai)
        ↓
Business (Kaam chahiye, pay karta hai)
```

**Tu kaam NAHI karega. Tu sirf platform chalayega. Dono side se paisa aayega.**

## 💰 TERA PAISA KAHAN KAHAN SE AAYEGA?

### Revenue Stream 1: Task Commission (Main Income)

```
Har task pe 15-20% commission:

₹500 ka task    → Tera: ₹75-100
₹2,000 ka task  → Tera: ₹300-400
₹10,000 ka task → Tera: ₹1,500-2,000

Agar din me 100 tasks complete hote hain (average ₹500):
100 × ₹75 = ₹7,500/din = ₹2,25,000/month 💰
```

### Revenue Stream 2: Worker Subscriptions

```
Workers ko zyada tasks chahiye → Wo PLAN lenge:

FREE:   5 tasks/day (limited)
₹299:   20 tasks/day
₹599:   Unlimited tasks + instant payout
₹999:   Everything + premium tools

Agar 5,000 workers me se 1,000 plan lete hain:
500 × ₹299 = ₹1,49,500
300 × ₹599 = ₹1,79,700
200 × ₹999 = ₹1,99,800
TOTAL = ₹5,29,000/month 💰
```

### Revenue Stream 3: Business Plans

```
Businesses ko premium features chahiye (bulk upload, 
verified workers, priority, analytics):

₹4,999/month  — Growth plan
₹9,999/month  — Enterprise plan

50 businesses × ₹4,999 = ₹2,49,950
20 businesses × ₹9,999 = ₹1,99,980
TOTAL = ₹4,49,930/month 💰
```

### Revenue Stream 4: Prompt Store (Marketplace Within Marketplace)

```
Workers apne prompts sell karte hain:

Worker sells prompt at ₹100
Platform takes 30% = ₹30 TERA
Worker gets 70% = ₹70

Daily 200 prompt sales × ₹30 = ₹6,000/din
= ₹1,80,000/month 💰
```

## 🎪 SIMPLE COMPARISON — SAMJHO AASANI SE

```
ZOMATO MODEL:
Restaurant banata hai → Zomato connect karta hai → Customer khata hai
Zomato commission: 20-30%

UBER MODEL:
Driver gaadi chalata hai → Uber connect karta hai → Rider travel karta hai
Uber commission: 25-30%

OLX MODEL:
Seller product deta hai → OLX connect karta hai → Buyer khareedta hai
OLX: listing fees leti hai

TASKPAISA MODEL:
Worker AI kaam karta hai → TaskPaisa connect karta hai → Business pay karta hai
TaskPaisa commission: 15-20%
```

**Tu wahi kar raha hai jo Zomato/Uber/OLX karta hai — sirf CONNECT kar raha hai, kaam koi aur karega, paiso ki katai teri hogi.**

## ✅ SUMMARY — 5 LINE ME POORA IDEA

1. **Businesses ko AI content chahiye** (images, videos, writing) — wo TASK post karenge aur PAY karenge
2. **Workers (students, housewives, freelancers)** wo tasks AI tools se complete karenge aur EARN karenge
3. **Tu (TaskPaisa)** sirf PLATFORM hai — connect karega, 15-20% commission khayega
4. **Worker lana EASY hai** — "ghar baithe paise kamao" sunke lakho log aayenge
5. **Business lana** — agencies, e-commerce sellers, brands ko approach kar — unka ₹50K/month ka kaam ₹15K me ho jayega

> **Tera kaam: Platform banana + Business laana + Quality maintain karna**
> **Baaki sab SYSTEM karega!**

🔄 Complete Flow
Ek Task Ka Poora Safar — Start to Finish
₹2,000 ka ek task kaise chalega — step by step dekhte hain.
STEP 01 — BUSINESS SIDE

🏢 Ravi (E-Commerce Seller) Task Post Karta Hai

Ravi ke paas 200 products hain. Professional images chahiye. Wo TaskPaisa pe aata hai aur task banata hai.

Task: "200 Product Images — White Background"
Budget: ₹2,000 (₹10/image)  |  Deadline: 3 days
STEP 02 — ESCROW LOCK

💰 ₹2,000 Platform Ke Paas Lock Hota Hai

Ravi ke ₹2,000 escrow me lock ho jaate hain. Matlab paise SAFE hain — na worker ko milenge abhi, na Ravi ko wapas (jab tak kaam complete na ho). Dono sides protected.

STEP 03 — TASK GOES LIVE

⚡ Task Marketplace Pe Live Ho Jata Hai

Ye task ab saare workers ko dikhega unke dashboard pe. Jo bhi eligible hai — wo accept kar sakta hai.

STEP 04 — WORKER SIDE

👩‍💻 Priya (College Student) Task Accept Karti Hai

Priya Delhi me rehti hai, Canva aur Remove.bg use jaanti hai. Wo ₹2,000 wala task accept karti hai.

STEP 05 — KAAM HOTA HAI

🎨 Priya AI Tools Se 200 Images Banati Hai

Remove.bg, Canva, Photoroom jaise AI tools use karke 2-3 ghante me 200 product images ready. Submit karti hai platform pe.

Time taken: 2-3 hours
Tools used: Remove.bg (free) + Canva (free) + Photoroom
STEP 06 — REVIEW

✅ Ravi Review Karke APPROVE Karta Hai

Ravi ko notification aata hai. Wo images check karta hai. Sab theek hai → APPROVE button dabata hai.

STEP 07 — PAYMENT RELEASE

💸 Paisa Split Hota Hai — SAB KHUSH!

Approve hote hi system automatically paisa split karta hai. Priya ke wallet me ₹1,700 aur tere account me ₹300.

🎯 Sabse Bada Sawal
Business Kaise Laayega? — 4 Phase Strategy
Pehle din se revenue kaise aayegi — step by step plan.
PHASE 01 — MONTH 1

🎭 TU Khud Dono Side Khel

Fiverr/Upwork se client le → TaskPaisa pe task post kar → Workers se karwa → Client ko deliver kar. Workers ko lagega "tasks aa rahe hain" — wo active rahenge. Tu directly bhi profit kamayega.

Example: Fiverr client ₹5,000 deta hai → Workers ₹2,000 me kaam karte hain → Tera profit: ₹3,000
Revenue: ₹50K - ₹1L/month
PHASE 02 — MONTH 2

📢 Agencies Ko Pakad

50 digital marketing agencies ko approach kar: "Apke designers ki ₹1-2L salary save hogi. Humpe ₹20-50K me same kaam." Pehle 5 tasks FREE de — result dikhega toh permanent buyer ban jayengi.

Target: 10 agencies × ₹30,000/month = ₹3,00,000/month
Revenue: ₹3-5L/month
PHASE 03 — MONTH 3

🛒 E-Commerce Sellers Ko Pakad

Amazon/Meesho seller groups me jaake bol: "₹10/image me professional product photos." Pehli 10 images FREE. Quality achhi lagi → monthly customer ban jayega.

Target: 100 sellers × ₹3,000/month = ₹3,00,000/month
Revenue: ₹5-10L/month
PHASE 04 — MONTH 4+

🤖 Automated Demand

"Done For You" packages launch kar: "Social Media Starter ₹2,999/month — 30 posts." Business ko task post bhi nahi karna padega — package select karo, system handle karega.

Plus: Shopify plugin → auto tasks, word-of-mouth, SEO traffic
Revenue: ₹15-50L/month
